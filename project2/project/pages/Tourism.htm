Discover the Hidden Charm of Sint-Michielsgestel

Welcome to Sint-Michielsgestel

A peaceful gem in the heart of North Brabant! Just minutes from Den Bosch, this lovely Dutch village is surrounded by rivers, forests, and endless green landscapes. Whether you are a nature lover, history enthusiast, or simply looking for a quiet escape from the city, Sint-Michielsgestel offers a perfect blend of relaxation, culture, and local adventure.

1. Landgoed Haanwijk - A Walk Through History

Step into a world of tranquility at Landgoed Haanwijk, a historic estate surrounded by lush forests and meadows. Wander through scenic walking trails, admire the old manor houses, and experience Dutch countryside at its finest. It is the perfect spot for a peaceful stroll, bird-watching, or a picnic by the river Dommel. Landgoed Haanwijk

2. Explore the Dommel River & Countryside

Discover the picturesque landscapes of Sint-Michielsgestel by bike or on foot. Follow the winding paths along the Dommel River, breathe in the fresh air, and stop by cozy cafés along the way. This is where the Netherlands shows its true charm — calm waters, wide skies, and friendly locals everywhere you go.

3. Kasteel Nieuw-Herlaer – Where the Past Comes Alive

Visit Kasteel Nieuw-Herlaer, an enchanting manor with centuries of history. Once a proud castle overlooking the Dommel, it still stands as a symbol of the region’s noble heritage. Wander around the estate, explore nearby Oud Herlaer ruins, and imagine life in medieval Brabant. Kasteel Nieuw-Herlaer

4. Local Events & Dutch Traditions

Experience authentic Dutch village life! From the Gestelse Winterfair to lively music events and holiday celebrations, there’s always something happening in Sint-Michielsgestel. Join the locals for a festival, try Dutch treats, and enjoy the warm hospitality that makes this village so special.

5. Kamelenmelkerij Smits – The Camel Dairy Adventure

Looking for something truly unique? Visit Kamelenmelkerij Smits, Europe’s only camel-milk farm! Meet friendly camels, learn how camel milk is produced, and even sample delicious camel-milk products. It’s a fun and educational experience for families, couples, and curious travelers alike. Kamelenmelkerij Smits
